FAQ

Questions we hear most

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Most clients describe a quick warm snap on the skin. Our systems cool the skin as they work, and we adjust settings to keep you comfortable.

How many sessions will I need?

Hair grows in cycles, so treatments are spaced several weeks apart. Most people need a series of sessions. We confirm a plan for you at your consultation.

Is it suitable for my skin tone?

We assess your skin and hair at your consultation and run a patch test before treatment, then choose settings that suit you.

How should I prepare?

Shave the area the day before, avoid sun exposure and waxing in the weeks leading up to your visit, and arrive with clean, product-free skin.
